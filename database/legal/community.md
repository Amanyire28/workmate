## 1. What the community is for

1.1 Sharing your work, skills, lessons, questions and opportunities with other people in the trades and services. Anyone can read posts. Members can post, comment, like and follow.

## 2. What WorkMate checks and what it doesn't

2.1 Posts are statements by the member, and WorkMate doesn't check them before they appear. Only the tick and job-based reviews reflect anything WorkMate itself has verified (Document 7). A post's claims about qualifications or results haven't been verified unless they say so.

## 3. Not allowed

- **False professional claims or misleading qualifications:** claiming licences, training or experience you don't have.
- **Stolen photos and copyright infringement:** using others' photos, designs or text as your own.
- **Defamation:** false statements that damage someone's reputation. Honest reviews of real jobs are fine.
- **Harassment, threats and bullying**, including repeated unwanted contact.
- **Discrimination and hate speech** on grounds such as tribe, religion, sex, disability or nationality.
- **Fraudulent advertising,** such as fake offers, "pay first" scams or pyramid schemes.
- **Impersonating** another person, business or WorkMate.
- **Sharing customer information:** names, addresses, phone numbers or details of a job without permission.
- **Photos of customers, their homes or premises** without their clear permission. Blur faces, house numbers and number plates, and never show children.
- **Dangerous or unlawful content,** such as instructions for illegal or unsafe work, or content promoting violence.
- **Spam:** repeated posts, irrelevant links or mass messaging.
- **Fake reviews and engagement manipulation:** buying, swapping or self-writing reviews, fake accounts, coordinated likes or follows.

## 4. Reporting

4.1 Use **Report** on any post, comment, profile or message. Tell us which rule was broken. If you report something as unlawful, include your name, what right was infringed and what you want done (ETA s.31(1)). We don't tell the person who reported them.

## 5. Moderation

5.1 Our moderators review reports and may:

- leave the content up;
- hide it;
- remove it;
- warn the member;
- restrict posting;
- suspend or remove the account.

We act faster on safety risks. We remove unlawful content within a reasonable time after we are told about it (ETA s.30(d)).

## 6. Appeals

6.1 If your content was removed or your account restricted, you can appeal within [[14]] days under **Account → Reports and complaints**. A moderator who wasn't involved reviews the appeal and replies within [[14]] days. Removed content is kept for [[90 days]] so it can be restored.
