## 1. Requesting a service

1.1 Send a request to a provider, or post a job that approved providers can apply for. Describe the job honestly and add up to 6 photos if they help. Before sending, you see a summary you can correct or cancel.

## 2. Comparing providers

2.1 Look at each provider's profile, reviews, tick and the places they serve. Ask questions in messages before hiring. Reviews are other customers' opinions, and profiles are the providers' own statements.

## 3. What the tick means

3.1 Grey: WorkMate accepted the provider's ID and documents when it reviewed them. Blue: the provider also met WorkMate's performance rules. Neither is a guarantee (Document 7).

## 4. Your safety

- Keep conversations and payment agreements in WorkMate.
- Share your exact address only once you've chosen a provider.
- Have someone with you, or tell someone, when a stranger comes to your home.
- Keep valuables away from the work area.
- Check the provider is the person on the profile.
- In an emergency, call the police on 999 or 112 first. [[confirm numbers]]

## 5. Agreeing the price

5.1 Prices are agreed between you and the provider. WorkMate doesn't set or approve them. Get a clear price before work starts, including materials, transport and any extras.

## 6. Quotations

6.1 Read what each quotation includes. You don't have to accept any of them. Ask for changes in writing.

## 7. Confirming the job

7.1 When the provider accepts, the job shows "in progress". Confirm the date, time, scope and price in messages. That status isn't a receipt.

## 8. Paying the provider

> WorkMate connects you with service providers. Payment for services is arranged directly between you and the provider. WorkMate does not collect or hold your service payment. Confirm the price and payment arrangement with your provider before work begins.

8.1 Pay only the provider you hired, by the method you agreed. Check the name on a mobile money account before sending, keep receipts and screenshots, and pay in stages for large jobs.

## 9. Cancelling

9.1 Cancel as early as possible through WorkMate. Document 6 explains what happens to deposits and work already done.

## 10. Changing the job

10.1 Agree any change to scope, price or timing in messages before the extra work is done.

## 11. Poor-quality work

11.1 Raise the problem with the provider first and give them a fair chance to fix it. The law requires reasonable care and skill and fit materials (SGSSA ss.16, 18). If it isn't resolved, report it with photos.

## 12. Complaints

12.1 Go to **Account → Reports and complaints**, or report from the provider's profile, a chat or the job. Choose a reason, describe what happened and add up to 3 photos. You can follow the status there.

## 13. Evidence

13.1 Useful evidence includes WorkMate messages, the quotation, payment receipts, before-and-after photos, and the names of witnesses. Never send us bank or mobile money PINs or passwords.

## 14. How disputes are handled

14.1 See Document 6. WorkMate reviews the evidence, may ask both sides questions, and can warn, restrict or remove accounts. We don't decide who owes money, and we don't pay compensation for a provider's work. You keep your rights to go to court or to the police.

## 15. Your conduct

15.1 Treat providers with respect, give a safe place to work, don't ask for unlawful or unsafe work, and pay what you agreed. Harassing a provider, or withholding agreed payment without reason, may lead to your account being restricted.

## 16. Your information and property

16.1 Providers see your name and photo, never your email or phone, unless you share them. Job photos are visible only to the providers involved and have location data removed. Keep personal documents and valuables out of photos and out of the work area.
