## 4. How we collect data

4.1 We collect data from you directly (DPPA s.11). We also collect from your use of WorkMate, and from other users when they send you a request, a review or a report about you. We don't buy data, and we don't read your phone contacts.

4.2 Giving the data marked "Contract" is necessary to use that feature. Without it, the feature won't work (DPPA s.13(1)(d)–(e)). Everything marked "Consent" is optional.

## 5. Storage and security

5.1 Our servers are in Uganda. We protect data with access permissions and encrypted connections (HTTPS). Private files such as verification documents, job photos and report evidence are stored outside public access. Photos are re-drawn to remove hidden data such as GPS position. Staff access to sensitive records is logged (DPPA s.20; DPP Regs 31–32).

5.2 Our processors must keep data confidential and secure under written contracts (DPPA ss.21–22).

## 6. Who we share data with

- Other users, as described in the table.
- Our processors: {{hosting_provider}}, {{email_provider}}, and browser push services (section 7).
- The authorities, where the law requires it or to prevent or detect a crime (DPPA s.7(2)(b)).
- A buyer of the business, under the same promises, if WorkMate is sold. We will tell you first.

We never sell personal data (DPPA s.37).

## 7. Transfers outside Uganda

7.1 WorkMate's servers are in Uganda. Some data leaves Uganda only in these cases:

- **Push notifications** pass through your browser maker's push service: Google, Apple or Mozilla, depending on your browser. The notification is short. Report outcomes and warnings never include their text.
- **Fonts and icons** currently load from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which see your device's IP address. [[remove this item once fonts and icons are hosted by WorkMate (planned)]]
- **Email delivery** [[if the email provider is outside Uganda]].

We rely on adequate protection in the receiving country or on your consent, given when you turn on push notifications, as DPPA s.19 and DPP Regs 30 require [[advocate]].

## 8. How long we keep data

8.1 We keep data only as long as the purpose needs, unless a law, a lawful purpose, a contract or your consent requires longer (DPPA s.18). Then we delete it so it can't be rebuilt, or remove the details that identify you (s.18(4)–(5)). The table gives each period. Data needed for an investigation, legal claim or court case is kept until that matter ends (s.18(2)).

## 9. Your rights

- **Access:** ask what we hold, why and who has seen it. We reply within 30 days (DPPA s.24; DPP Regs 35). You can also use **Download my data** any time.
- **Correction and deletion:** fix or remove data that is wrong, out of date, excessive or unlawfully held (s.16; Regs 29, 39).
- **Objection:** ask us to stop processing that causes you substantial damage or distress, or that doesn't fit the purpose we collected it for. We reply within 14 days (s.25; Reg 36).
- **Marketing:** stop it at any time, free. We confirm within 14 days (s.26; ETA s.26).
- **Automated decisions:** ask that a decision significantly affecting you isn't made by a computer alone (s.27; Reg 38). WorkMate's suspensions and verification decisions are made by people.

To use a right, go to **Account → Privacy** or email {{privacy_email}}. We may ask you to prove your identity first (DPPA s.24(1); DPP Regs 35(2)).

## 10. Withdrawing consent

10.1 You can withdraw any consent in Settings as easily as you gave it. This doesn't affect what we did before you withdrew. We keep a record of when you gave and withdrew consent.

## 11. Complaints

11.1 Complain to our DPO first at {{privacy_email}}. We aim to respond within [[14]] days. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Office at {{pdpo_contact}} (DPPA s.31), or apply to court for compensation (s.33).

## 12. Data breaches

12.1 If we believe someone has accessed your data without permission, we tell the Personal Data Protection Office immediately, with what happened and what we are doing (DPPA s.23; DPP Regs 33). We tell you directly when the Office decides we should, by email, in the app or on our website, with enough detail for you to protect yourself.

## 13. Children

13.1 WorkMate is for adults only. We don't knowingly collect data about children (DPPA s.8). Don't post photos or details of children in the community or in job photos.

## 14. Analytics and diagnostics

14.1 We count daily use, searches and profile views, mostly as totals without names. An anonymous install ID tells us how many people installed the app. We use no third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If that ever changes, we will ask for your consent first.

## 15. Cookies and browser storage

15.1 We use only what WorkMate needs to work:

- a session cookie, to keep you signed in;
- a security token (XSRF-TOKEN), against forged requests;
- a "keep me signed in" cookie, if you choose it;
- browser storage for your chosen area, the push reminder and the anonymous install ID.

The cookies are marked secure and can't be read by scripts where possible. There are no advertising cookies.

## 16. Marketing

16.1 Marketing messages are off unless you turn them on. Service messages, such as security alerts, request updates and changes to these documents, aren't marketing and are always sent. Every marketing email has a free unsubscribe link (ETA s.26(1)).

## 17. Closing your account

17.1 When you close your account:

- your profile is removed;
- your reviews and community posts are deleted or shown as "Former member" [[choose]];
- everything else follows the periods in the table.

Messages you sent stay visible to the person you sent them to, under "Former member", until their own retention ends.

## 18. What the law makes us keep

18.1 We keep some records because a law requires it or for legal claims. Examples are security and audit logs, legal acceptance records, and records needed by the authorities [[advocate: tax and limitation periods]].

## 19. Changes to this policy

19.1 If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we explain the change first. If we want to use your data for a new purpose that needs consent, we ask you separately. We never treat an old consent as covering a new purpose.
