## 1. Applying

1.1 You apply through WorkMate with your profession, skills, the places you serve and your documents. We may approve, ask for more, or decline. We tell you the reason if we decline.

## 2. Your profile

2.1 Everything on your profile must be true and kept current, including your name, business name, photos, prices, experience and availability. Only use photos of your own work. Remove anything that stops being true.

## 3. Qualifications and licences

3.1 You must hold every licence, registration and permit your work legally needs, such as trade or professional registration and any local authority permits, and keep them valid. Only offer work you are qualified and competent to do. Tell us if a licence lapses or is withdrawn.

## 4. Verification documents

4.1 You must provide your National ID and the documents your profession needs, and they must be genuine and yours. Fake or altered documents lead to permanent removal and may be reported to the police. We keep replaced documents as a record (Document 7).

## 5. Accepting work

5.1 You are free to accept or decline any request. Declining doesn't lower your ranking. When you decline, give a short reason so the customer can look elsewhere.

## 6. Talking to customers

6.1 Use WorkMate messages to agree the job, so there is a record. Be clear and polite. Never ask a customer for personal information the job doesn't need.

## 7. Prices and quotations

7.1 You set your own prices. A quotation must state the work included, the price, materials, any deposit, the expected timing and how long the offer stands. Don't raise the price after agreement unless the customer agrees to a change in scope.

## 8. Quality of work

8.1 You must carry out work with reasonable care and skill, and use materials that are sound and reasonably fit for their purpose. The law implies these terms into your agreement with the customer, and you can't exclude them (SGSSA ss.16, 18, 19).

## 9. Turning up on time

9.1 Arrive when agreed. If you will be late or can't come, tell the customer through WorkMate as early as you can. Repeated no-shows lead to restrictions (Document 6).

## 10. Safety

10.1 Work safely. Use proper protective equipment, follow the rules for your trade, and stop work that becomes unsafe. Never use a child to work for you, and never do hazardous work you aren't qualified for (Document 8). [[advocate: Occupational Safety and Health Act duties]]

## 11. Tools and equipment

11.1 You provide your own tools and equipment unless you agree otherwise with the customer, and you keep them safe and in good order.

## 12. Materials

12.1 Agree in writing who buys the materials and how they are paid for. Keep receipts. Return unused materials the customer paid for.

## 13. Customer property

13.1 Treat the customer's home or premises with care. Only use the areas you need. Ask before moving or using anything, and leave the place clean and secure.

## 14. Confidentiality

14.1 Information you learn about a customer must be used only for the job and kept confidential, both during and after it. That includes their address, phone, home, family and routines (DPPA s.35; Terms clause 27). Don't post photos of customers or their premises without their clear permission (Document 5).

## 15. Insurance

15.1 WorkMate doesn't insure you, your work or customers. Consider insurance suitable for your trade, and get it where the law or the customer requires it. [[advocate: any compulsory cover by trade]]

## 16. Damage and negligence

16.1 You are responsible for loss or damage caused by your negligence or your breach of the agreement with the customer. That includes damage caused by anyone you bring to the job.

## 17. Theft and misconduct

17.1 Theft, fraud, violence, threats or harassment lead to immediate suspension, permanent removal and, where appropriate, a report to the police. WorkMate cooperates with lawful requests from the authorities.

## 18. Complaints about you

18.1 Customers may report you. A report is an allegation until the safety team acts on it. We'll ask for your side before deciding anything that affects your account, except where urgent safety action is needed.

## 19. Cancellations

19.1 Follow Document 6. If you cancel a job you accepted, tell the customer straight away and return any deposit for work you won't do.

## 20. Payments come straight from customers

20.1 Customers pay you directly. WorkMate doesn't collect, hold or pass on payments, and doesn't guarantee that a customer will pay. Give clear payment instructions and receipts where required, and only accept payment for work agreed through WorkMate in the way you agreed.

## 21. Tax

21.1 You are responsible for your own tax, including income tax, VAT if you are registered, and any local service tax, and for keeping your own records. WorkMate doesn't deduct tax or report your earnings, because it never handles them. [[advocate: URA position]]

## 22. If you employ people

22.1 If you employ people or bring helpers, you are their employer. You are responsible for their pay, safety and conduct, and for your obligations under the Employment Act (Cap. 226) and the NSSF Act. Helpers must be adults.

22.2 Nothing in this agreement makes you WorkMate's employee, agent or partner. WorkMate doesn't control how, when or for whom you work. Annex B explains how the law decides who is an employee.

## 23. Restrictions and removal

23.1 We may give a warning, hide your profile, suspend or remove your account for breaches, using the steps in Document 8. We tell you why and how to ask for a review.

## 24. Disputes

24.1 Disputes with customers follow Document 6. Disputes with WorkMate follow Terms clause 31.
