## 1. Prohibited activities

- **Fraud and theft:** fake jobs, advance-fee scams, false payment claims, stealing from customers or providers.
- **Threats and violence:** any threat, intimidation or physical harm.
- **Harassment,** including sexual harassment and unwanted contact after a job.
- **Exploitation:** unfair pressure on vulnerable people, or withholding pay or documents.
- **Forced labour and human trafficking:** any work under threat or debt bondage, or recruitment for exploitation. [[advocate: Prevention of Trafficking in Persons Act 2009 citation]]
- **Child labour:** using anyone under 18 to carry out work booked through WorkMate, and any hazardous work by children (Employment Act Cap. 226; Employment (Employment of Children) Regulations 2012) [[advocate: sections]].
- **Discrimination** in hiring, pricing or service on grounds such as tribe, religion, sex or disability.
- **Unsafe work:** ignoring safety rules, or working without the competence or protective equipment the job needs.
- **Illegal services:** anything unlawful in Uganda, or work that needs a licence the provider doesn't have.
- **Impersonation** of a person, business or WorkMate staff.
- **Misuse of customer or provider information:** using contact details or addresses for anything other than the job, collecting or selling them, or stalking (DPPA ss.35, 37).
- **Unlawful content** (Document 5).
- **Platform manipulation:** fake accounts, fake reviews, scraping, automated access, getting around a suspension, or attacking WorkMate's systems.

## 2. How to report

2.1 Report from the profile, chat, job or post, or under **Account → Reports and complaints**. In danger, contact the police first on [[999 / 112]], then tell us.

## 3. What happens next

1. Received: we confirm.
2. Being looked at: we review the evidence and may restrict the account straight away for safety.
3. Your answer needed: we may ask the reporter or the reported person questions.
4. Decision: no action, warning, restriction, suspension or removal.
5. Review: either side may ask for a review within [[14]] days.

3.1 For serious crimes, we keep the evidence and cooperate with the police when lawfully asked.
