## 3. Definitions and interpretation

- **Customer** means a person who uses WorkMate to find, request or hire a service.
- **Provider** means a person or business approved by WorkMate to offer services on WorkMate.
- **Business** means a provider profile run by more than one person. Each person is a **member** of the business.
- **Request** means a message from a customer asking a provider to do a job.
- **Posted job** means a job a customer publishes for approved providers to apply for.
- **Quotation** means a price or offer a provider sends in reply.
- **Booking** means a request a provider has accepted (shown as "in progress").
- **Service Agreement** means the agreement between a customer and a provider about a job.
- **Content** means anything you post, send or upload. This includes profiles, photos, posts, comments, messages, reviews and reports.
- **Tick** means the verification mark described in Document 7.
