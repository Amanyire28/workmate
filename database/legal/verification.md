## 1. What we check

1.1 Providers send their National ID (front and back) and up to 5 supporting documents, such as certificates, trade test results, permits or letters. Each profession has its own list of expected documents.

## 2. Documents set per profession

2.1 WorkMate's administrators set and update the list for each profession. When the list changes, existing approvals stand unless we tell the provider otherwise.

## 3. How we review

3.1 A trained reviewer looks at each document for readability, consistency with the profile, and signs of alteration. We don't contact issuing bodies unless the document says we have [[confirm whether any registry checks are done]]. Every time a document is opened, it is logged.

## 4. Approval

4.1 When every document is accepted and nothing is outstanding, the provider gets the grey tick.

## 5. Rejection

5.1 We tell the provider which document was rejected and why, and they can send a new one.

## 6. Asking for more

6.1 A reviewer may ask for further documents, such as a permit the profession requires. The tick isn't given until they arrive.

## 7. Expired documents

7.1 Providers must replace documents before they expire. We may remove the tick if a required document has expired.

## 8. Fake documents

8.1 Using a fake or someone else's document means permanent removal, keeping the records, and possibly a report to the police.

## 9. Suspension

9.1 We may remove a tick or suspend a provider while we look into a serious report or doubts about a document.

## 10. Appeals

10.1 A provider can ask for any verification decision to be reviewed within [[14]] days. Reopening a decision needs a senior reviewer.

## 11. The grey tick

11.1 It means WorkMate reviewed and accepted the provider's identity document and supporting documents on the date of review. It doesn't mean WorkMate certifies their skills, holds their licence, guarantees their work, has checked their criminal record, or insures them.

## 12. The blue tick

12.1 It is shown instead of the grey tick when a verified provider meets WorkMate's performance rules, for example completed jobs and customer ratings [[final criteria]]. It reflects past results on WorkMate and isn't a guarantee of future work. It is reviewed regularly and can be removed. Providers can't buy it.

## 13. What a tick can never promise

13.1 No verification can rule out future fraud, negligence or misconduct. Always follow the safety steps in Document 4 and report any concern.
